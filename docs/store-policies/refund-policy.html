Return and Refund Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy applies to purchases made directly from the FretFuel online store, operated by RISING INDUSTRIES LLC. It describes our voluntary return program as well as how to contact us about an order problem. Rights and remedies provided by applicable consumer law continue to apply.

1. Returns within 30 days

For a change of mind or an incorrectly selected part, email fretfuel1@gmail.com within 30 calendar days after delivery to request a return. Please include your order number, the item you wish to return, and the reason for your request.

For these voluntary returns, the item must be unused, uninstalled, unmodified, and in a condition suitable for resale, with its original packaging, accessories, and included components. Parts that have been soldered, cut, drilled, fitted, or otherwise altered are not eligible for a change-of-mind return. These conditions do not exclude a valid claim concerning a defect, an incorrect description, or an error by FretFuel.

2. Confirm fit before installation

Please compare the product specifications with your instrument before ordering or installing a part. Relevant details may include dimensions, mounting-hole positions, string spacing, shaft or post diameter, orientation, and electrical specifications. A similar appearance or model name alone does not establish compatibility. If you are unsure, contact us before installation. If our listing is inaccurate or we supply the wrong item, we will handle the issue as a seller error rather than a change-of-mind return.

4. Return costs

For an eligible change-of-mind return, you pay the return-shipping cost. Original shipping charges are generally not refunded for these returns, except where required by law. We do not charge a restocking fee.

If an item is defective, damaged on arrival, materially different from its description, or incorrectly supplied by us, we will cover reasonable authorized return-shipping costs when a return is needed. We will also refund original shipping charges attributable to the affected item where appropriate or required by law. Contact us before purchasing a return label so that we can arrange a suitable method or agree on reimbursement.

5. Damaged, defective, incorrect, or missing items

Tell us promptly if your order has a problem. Give us your order number and a description of the issue; photographs of the item, packaging, and shipping label can help us investigate. An unboxing video is not a condition of making a claim. Keep the item and packaging where reasonably possible while we arrange the next steps.

We will assess the problem and arrange an appropriate remedy, which may include a replacement or refund, taking account of your rights under applicable law. The 30-day voluntary return window and the unused-item conditions above do not limit statutory rights or remedies for defects discovered later. Damage caused by misuse or an installation error is distinct from a defect present in the product; we will consider the circumstances of each claim.

6. Refunds and exchanges

We will let you know the outcome after reviewing your request and, when a return is needed, inspecting the returned item. Approved refunds will be submitted to the original payment method within 10 business days after approval, or sooner if applicable law requires. Your payment provider may need additional time to display the credit. Contact us if an approved refund has not appeared after that period.

For a voluntary exchange, contact us about returning the original item and ordering the replacement separately. This does not affect any right to a replacement or other remedy for a defective or incorrectly supplied item.

7. Cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible to request cancellation or an address correction. We will try to accommodate your request before shipment, but cannot guarantee that an order already in fulfillment can be stopped. If an order has shipped, the return provisions above apply, subject to any additional cancellation rights provided by law. If we cannot fulfill an order, we will notify you and promptly refund amounts paid for the unfulfilled items and related shipping charges.
