Shipping Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

FretFuel is operated by RISING INDUSTRIES LLC. This policy explains shipping arrangements for orders from our online store.

1. Destinations and availability

Our delivery regions are the United States, Canada, and supported countries in South America. Service is available only for destinations and addresses with an enabled shipping option at checkout. Availability can vary by product, carrier, weight, and local restrictions. If your address has no available shipping option, contact us before ordering; a country being within one of these regions does not guarantee service to every address.

2. Shipping origin

Orders normally ship from our U.S. warehouse at 10509 Business Drive, Fontana, CA 92337, USA. This is a fulfillment location. Any collection or return must be arranged with customer service in advance; please do not visit or send goods there without instructions.

3. Shipping charges and currency

Available delivery methods and shipping charges are shown at checkout before you pay. Charges can depend on the destination, package weight, order contents, and selected service. Check the currency displayed at checkout. Free shipping applies only when expressly offered and when the order meets the stated conditions.

4. Dispatch and delivery estimates

Order preparation and carrier transit are separate stages. Any dispatch or delivery estimate stated on the product page, at checkout, or in an order-specific communication applies to that order. Where no estimate is displayed, contact us before ordering for information about the relevant destination and service. We do not state a fixed processing period or a universal delivery period in this policy.

Transit estimates are not guaranteed delivery dates unless a service is expressly sold with a guarantee. Weekends, public holidays, address problems, weather, carrier disruption, and customs clearance may affect timing. If we cannot ship within a stated shipment period or an applicable legal deadline, we will notify you and seek your agreement to a revised shipment arrangement or cancel the unshipped portion and provide a prompt refund. This policy does not permit indefinite delay or remove your cancellation or refund rights.

5. Canadian and South American orders

Shipments outside the United States may require customs clearance and accurate recipient details. Import duties, import taxes, brokerage charges, and other destination fees may apply. Unless we expressly state that these charges are included or collect them at checkout, they are not included in the product price or shipping charge and are the recipient's responsibility, subject to applicable law. Customs clearance may add time to delivery.

Before ordering, check any local restrictions and information requirements for the products you are buying. If a shipment is refused or returned because of an address, customs, or import issue, contact us so that we can review available options and any documented, legally recoverable costs. We do not automatically treat every such order as non-refundable.

6. Shipping addresses and changes

Provide a complete and accurate address and any information required by the selected carrier. Email us promptly if a correction is needed. A change may not be possible once fulfillment or transit has begun. If an address issue causes a return, we will explain any reasonable reshipping cost before arranging another shipment.

7. Tracking and delivery problems

When your shipment has tracking, we will provide the tracking details using the order contact information. Tracking updates can take time to appear. If tracking says delivered but you cannot find the package, check with other household members or building staff and the carrier, then contact us. For a damaged package, missing item, incorrect item, or suspected loss, send us your order number and relevant details promptly.

We will work with you and the carrier to investigate and arrange an appropriate resolution. A carrier delivery scan does not by itself remove your rights, and handing a package to a carrier does not automatically release us from obligations imposed by applicable law.

8. Returns and contact

Returns are covered by our Return and Refund Policy. Obtain return instructions before shipping goods back.
